# AssemblyAI Voice Agent Hackathon brief

> Information checked on September 1, 2026. Event-specific rules are still evolving; check official pages again before submission. This document is a translation of that dated brief, not a new verification of the rules.

## Objective

Build and deploy a usable voice agent with **AssemblyAI** within one month, and submit a complete demonstration package before the deadline.

## Event background

- Organizers: lablab.ai and AssemblyAI.
- Format: a global, month-long online event; individuals and teams may participate for free.
- Theme: the fastest path to a working voice agent. Every project must use AssemblyAI.
- Dates: September 1–30, 2026.
- Prize pool: **USD 10,000** in total, split between USD 5,000 in cash and USD 5,000 in AssemblyAI credits. Allocation by placement has not been announced.
- A company email is recommended, but a personal email can be used to register and submit.

At the time of this brief, AssemblyAI offers an integrated Voice Agent API that takes speech input and returns speech output over a single WebSocket, combining recognition, noise reduction, turn and interruption detection, reasoning, speech synthesis, and tool calling. It is a route to a quick end-to-end prototype, but the event page has not specified a required API or model.

## Confirmed core requirements

1. Use AssemblyAI and deliver a working voice agent application.
2. Submit online within the event period. Every team member should register individually on lablab.ai.
3. Even solo participants need to create or join a team on the platform.
4. Provide a publicly accessible, interactive deployed prototype, not only a concept or local demo.
5. Plagiarism, cheating, vote manipulation, unauthorized automation, or other conduct that undermines fairness may result in disqualification.

## Deadlines

- Kickoff: September 1, 2026, at 15:00 UTC, or **23:00 China Standard Time (UTC+8)**.
- Submission: September 30, 2026, at 15:00 UTC, or **23:00 China Standard Time (UTC+8)**.
- General platform rules permit manual submission within six hours after the deadline only for technical problems, with reasonable justification and prior organizer or mentor approval. Do not treat this as a routine grace period.

> Registration information conflicts: the event description says participants may join throughout the build period, while the live page says registration closes at kickoff. Follow the stricter interpretation and register and form a team early.

## General submission checklist

The following comes from lablab.ai general rules. Event-specific rules take precedence if they differ.

- Project name, short description, and full description.
- Technology tags and project category.
- A 16:9 PNG or JPG cover image.
- An MP4 demonstration or project presentation.
- A PDF pitch deck.
- A public GitHub repository.
- A deployed, publicly accessible application URL. General rules list Streamlit, Replit, and Vercel as deployment examples.
- Descriptions within platform character and word limits, and links accessible without owner sign-in or from the judges' environment.

## General judging criteria

- **Presentation:** clarity of the problem, solution, demonstration, and communication.
- **Business value:** intended users, practical value, and commercial viability.
- **Application of technology:** meaningful use of AssemblyAI in the core experience and reliable application behavior.
- **Originality:** a distinctive solution rather than simply adding voice to an existing product.

Event-specific weights have not been announced. Prioritize a complete, stable, easy-to-understand voice interaction before expanding features.
